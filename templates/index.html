How it works

One clear path from complaint to change.

Six steps. No maze. The citizen reports, the AI understands, institutions accept, and together they build verified impact.

01
Report

Citizen submits problem title, description, location, category, and uploads images or videos.

02
AI Understands

AI analyzes category, severity, priority, required skills, technologies, and evidence relevance.

03
AI Finds the Right Institution

AI compares the problem against departments, research areas, faculty, labs, facilities, technologies, and capacity.

04
Institution Accepts

Institution receives the challenge with match score, reasons, and can accept or decline with explanation.

05
Project & Milestones

Assigned institution creates milestones, updates progress, uploads evidence, and tracks to deployment.

06
Verification & Impact

Citizen verifies the solution. Government measures resolution rate, impact, and institutional performance.
