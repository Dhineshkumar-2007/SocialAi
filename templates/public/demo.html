Social AI AI · End-to-end flow

A guided walkthrough of the full ecosystem: from citizen report through AI analysis, institution acceptance, project workspace, and verification.

01 — Report

Citizen reports a problem

A citizen reports broken streetlights near a government school in Trichy. The report includes title, description, location, category, and photographic evidence.

Report a Problem
02 — AI Understands

Classification, evidence intelligence, duplicate check

AI extracts category (Infrastructure), severity (HIGH), priority (87), required skills (IoT, Embedded Systems, Computer Vision), and checks image evidence quality (94%) and consistency (89%).

03 — Best institution

AI matches and explains

Using expertise, labs, previous projects, faculty, and capacity — the matcher ranks ABC Engineering University at 91% with clear explanations.

Institution challenges
04 — Accept & build

Institution accepts, creates milestones

The institution reviews the challenge, accepts, creates milestones (Investigation → Design → Prototype → Testing → Deployment), and updates progress with evidence.

Project workspace
05 — Citizen tracks

Simplified view for the citizen

The citizen sees progress (78%), current stage (Field Testing), latest update from the university, and knows what comes next — pilot deployment.

Track progress
06 — Verify

Citizen verification closes the loop

After deployment, the citizen verifies: YES (solved), PARTIALLY, or NO (problem reopened with notification to institution and government). Evidence can be uploaded.
